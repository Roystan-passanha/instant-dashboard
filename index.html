How it works

  1. 01

    Drop a file

    Excel or CSV. Messy dates, numbers stored as text and spelling variants are fixed before anything is charted.

  2. 02

    Answer a few questions

    Which number matters most? What do you want to compare by? Each answer redraws the dashboard.

  3. 03

    Read what stands out

    Headline numbers, charts that fit the data, and plain sentences about the largest share, the trend and unusual rows.

Your file stays on your computer

The file is read by your own browser. It is not sent to a server, and nothing is kept after you close the tab.
